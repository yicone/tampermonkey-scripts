在多家以对话 AI 为主的产品官网同步提交同一句提问，并提供提问与回答的目录导航。各站仍用自己的模型来回答（其中也可能包含音视频或多模态能力）。

本脚本基于 interest2 的 [多模型同时回答 & 目录导航](https://greasyfork.org/scripts/537302)（5.2.6）。感谢原作者的工作。

This userscript submits the same prompt across conversational AI sites and adds a table of contents for questions and answers. Each site still answers with its own models (which may include audio, video, or other multimodal models).

It is based on interest2's [Multi-model simultaneous answering & directory navigation](https://greasyfork.org/scripts/537302) (5.2.6). Thanks to the original author.

Source: https://github.com/yicone/tampermonkey-scripts
